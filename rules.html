    Server Rules

    The full rules, with what each one covers. /rules in game shows the short version. Staff have the final say on anything these don't cover.

    Penalties. Breaking a rule can lead to a warning, a mute, a kick or a ban, depending on how serious it is and whether it's happened before.

    1. Be respectful#

    Treat every player and staff member with kindness. Arguments happen. Keep them calm, and walk away if one gets heated. Take long disagreements to private messages instead of public chat.

    2. No harassment#

    Harassment of any kind isn't allowed. That covers bullying, threats, hate speech, stalking, following someone around after they've asked you to stop, and picking on anyone for who they are. This applies in game, in private messages and on the Discord.

    3. Keep chat clean#

    Tenko is for all ages. No slurs, no sexual or graphic talk, and go easy on swearing. This includes names, nicknames, signs, books, item names, shop names and builds, not just chat.

    4. No spam#

    Don't flood chat, repeat the same message or type in all caps. Don't advertise other servers, websites, social media or channels.

    5. No griefing#

    Don't break, change or take anything that isn't yours. That goes for builds, chests, farms, crops, animals, pets and shops, whether they're claimed or not. Unclaimed doesn't mean free. If you aren't sure whether something belongs to someone, ask first.

    Killing someone's pets or tamed animals, luring mobs into a base, or building right up against someone's claim to block their view or entrance all count.

    6. Play fair#

    No hacked clients, x-ray texture packs or mods, auto-clickers, macros, or anything else that gives an unfair edge. Mods that come in the TMC Modpack are fine. If you're unsure about a client-side mod you want to add, ask staff first.

    Found a bug or a dupe? Tell staff and don't use it. Using one, or keeping quiet about it while others do, is treated the same as cheating.

    7. Trade honestly#

    No scamming. Keep your end of every deal and be clear about what you're offering. If you agree to a price, pay it. Staff can't undo a trade, so for anything big, use a player shop or the auction house, where the item and the coins change hands together.

    8. Stay private#

    Never share anyone's personal information: real names, photos, addresses, schools, or where they live. Keep your own safe too. Nobody on the server needs any of it.

    9. Respect staff#

    Follow staff directions. Don't pretend to be staff. If you think a decision was unfair, talk to staff calmly in private or on the Discord. Arguing about it in public chat won't change the outcome.

    PvP#

    PvP is on. If you don't want to fight, type /pvp off. With PvP off, other players can't hurt you and you can't hurt them. /pvp on turns it back on.

    • You can switch once every 30 seconds.
    • You can't switch in the 15 seconds after you hit or were hit by a player, so turning it off mid-fight doesn't work.
    • Killing a player who has PvP on is allowed. Spawn-camping, repeatedly killing the same player, or using PvP to get around the griefing rule isn't.

    10. Common sense#

    If something feels wrong, it probably is. A rule not being listed doesn't make it allowed. When in doubt, ask a member of staff.

    Reporting a problem#

    If someone breaks a rule, message a staff member in game or on the Discord. Screenshots help, and the coordinates if it's griefing. Don't take it into your own hands.
