    How Tenko Works

    Tenko MC is a modded survival server on Minecraft 1.21.1 and NeoForge. This section of the wiki covers the server itself: money, ranks, land, rewards, travel and the rules. For what each mod adds, see the Mod Wiki.

    Your first hour#

    1. Talk to Natsubashi, the Tenko Guardian, at spawn. He gives you the Book of Knowledge, the starter kit (iron armor and tools, bread, 2 golden apples, 2 Waystones), 30 Bronze, 10 Silver, 2 Gold and a stack of torches.
    2. Read the rules with /rules, or the full version on the Rules page.
    3. Look around spawn. The shops, Clucky's coin exchange, Rufus, the five crates and the WarpGates to the resource worlds are all here.
    4. Jump into the water at the "Jump into action here!" sign. It drops you at a random spot in the main world.
    5. Find a spot and claim it. Run /claim deed, hold the Claim Deed and right-click to claim the chunk you're standing in. See Claims.
    6. Set a home with /sethome base. There's no /spawn, /tpa or /back, so homes and Waystones are how you get around.
    7. Vote with /vote. Every vote is a Vote Coin and counts toward your next rank.

    How it fits together#

    Most of the server runs on coins. You earn them from Rufus for time played, from other players through trades, shops and the auction house, and from loot bags dropped by mobs and bosses. You spend them in the spawn shops or on other players' goods.

    Crates are separate. They don't take Bronze, Silver or Gold. Each one opens with its own coin, found in loot chests, dropped by bosses, won from a lower crate, or earned by voting.

    Ranks come from votes only. They can't be bought. Each rank adds one home and 100 claim chunks, and changes your name color in chat and the tab list.

    Titles are cosmetic. Title Scrolls drop from chests and bosses, and /title picks the one shown next to your name.

    Good to know#

    • Death: your items go into a gravestone where you died. Go back and break it to get everything back. Only you and staff can break it.
    • AFK: nobody gets kicked for being AFK. /afk marks you away, and you're marked automatically after a while. AFK time doesn't count toward playtime rewards.
    • Nightly restart: the server restarts every night at 23:30 server time, with warnings from 30 minutes out.
    • Loot chests are personal. Everyone who opens a dungeon or structure chest gets their own loot, so there's no rush to get there first.
    • Elytra can't be used at spawn.
    • Phantoms can be turned off for yourself with /phantoms off.
    • Discord: discord.gg/GGQyUzt2Rq. Chat is bridged both ways, and /link connects your Discord account so your rank role follows you.
