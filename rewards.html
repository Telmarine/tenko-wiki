    Playtime & Voting

    Two things pay you for being part of the server: time spent playing, collected from Rufus, and votes on the server lists, which give Vote Coins and ranks.

    Playtime rewards#

    Rufus, The Timekeepa at spawn tracks how long you've played. There are 20 reward tiers, from 1 hour up to 128 hours. Right-click Rufus and he hands over everything you've earned and haven't collected yet, all at once, then tells you how long until the next one.

    • Only active time counts. If you stand still too long, your playtime clock pauses until you move again.
    • Nothing expires. Skip Rufus for a week and the rewards wait for you.
    • Reaching a tier is announced in chat.

    Voting#

    Type /vote for clickable links to the five vote sites:

    • Modded MC Servers
    • Minecraft Servers
    • Minecraft Buzz
    • MC Server List
    • Minecraft MP

    You can vote on each one once a day, so up to five votes a day. Use your exact in-game name on the vote site. Each vote:

    • gives you a Vote Coin, which opens the Vote Crate
    • adds 1 to your vote total, which decides your rank

    At five votes a day, Ichibi takes 5 days and Tenko rank about six months.
