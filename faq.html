    FAQ

    Answers to the questions that come up most in game and on the Discord.

    Getting started#

    How do I join?

    Install the TMC Modpack (see the Install Guide), launch it, and add play.tenkominecraft.com under Multiplayer.

    I get a "missing mod" or "mod mismatch" error.

    Your modpack is out of date. Download the newest one from the Discord and import it again. The server runs TMC Modpack 1.2 on NeoForge 21.1.255.

    I lost my Book of Knowledge.

    Everything in it is on this wiki. If you still want a copy, ask a staff member.

    Getting around#

    How do I get back to spawn?

    There's no /spawn. Set a home at spawn before you leave, or use a Waystone.

    Why can't I /tpa to my friend?

    Teleport requests are off on purpose. Meet up with Waystones, or have your friend set a Waystone at their base for you to activate.

    I can't set a home here.

    Homes can't be set in the resource worlds. You may also be at your home limit. /homes shows how many you have.

    How big is the world?

    The main Overworld is 25,000 × 25,000 blocks, centered on 0,0. The Nether border sits at 3,125 and the End border at 10,000.

    Money and trading#

    How do I make money early on?

    Collect from Rufus after your first hour, sell spare farm goods to other players or through the auction house, and kill mobs for loot bags. Once you have a few Silver, set up a chest shop.

    Can I sell items to the NPC shops?

    No. They only sell. Sell to players instead, with a chest shop or the auction house.

    What's the difference between my coins and my balance?

    Coins are items in your inventory. Your balance is money held by the server, used for /pay. /exchange deposit and /exchange withdraw move money between the two.

    I typed a price and it came out wrong.

    Prices are in Bronze. 1 Gold is 10000 or 10k. Don't add a coin letter: 5b means 5 billion.

    Someone scammed me. Can staff get my stuff back?

    Report it with screenshots. Scamming breaks the rules and staff will deal with the player, but trades can't always be undone. Use chest shops or the auction house for big trades.

    Crates, rewards and ranks#

    How do I open a crate?

    Hold the matching crate coin and right-click the crate. The Crates page lists every prize.

    Where do I find crate coins?

    Vote Coins come from voting. Sapphire Star, Dark Emerald, Onyx and Tenko Coins turn up in loot chests, Boss Loot Bags and lower crates.

    Rufus says I have nothing to collect.

    Only active time counts. Standing still pauses the clock, so AFK time doesn't add up. Rufus tells you how long until the next tier.

    I voted but didn't get anything.

    Check that you typed your exact in-game name on the vote site. Votes sometimes take a few minutes to come through. If it still hasn't arrived after an hour, tell staff which site and roughly when.

    Can I buy a rank?

    No. Ranks come from votes only.

    What is a Custom Armor Set or Custom Weapon Ticket?

    The rarest prizes in the Tenko Crate. Give the ticket to x_StormRage_x and he makes you a one-of-a-kind armor set or weapon.

    Land and building#

    Someone built right next to my base.

    Claims need a 2-chunk gap from other players' claims, so nobody can claim right against yours. Claim the area around your base early if you want space. Unclaimed land near you is fair game for building, but griefing your build isn't.

    My friend can't open my chests.

    Trust them with /trust <name>, then check which rank they're in under /claim → Ranks and that the rank can open containers.

    Are claims removed if I stop playing?

    No. Claims don't expire on Tenko.

    Can mobs and explosions damage my claim?

    That's up to you. Explosions and fire spread are flags in Land Config.

    Other#

    Why is there a gravestone where I died?

    It holds everything you were carrying. Break it to get it all back. Only you and staff can break it.

    Phantoms keep attacking me.

    Type /phantoms off.

    How do I show off an item in chat?

    Hold it and type [item] in your message, or shift + right-click it in your inventory.

    When does the server restart?

    Every night at 23:30 server time. You'll get warnings at 30, 15, 10 and 5 minutes, then 1 minute and 30 seconds.
