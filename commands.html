    Guide / Reference

    Player Commands

    Every command a player can use on Tenko, grouped by the mod it comes from. /help in game lists the ones open to you. Staff-only commands aren't listed.

    Reading this page. <angle brackets> are required, [square brackets] are optional. Don't type the brackets.

    Elements: Nexus#

    The server's core. It runs ranks, chat, the tab list and the side panel, plus homes, kits, private messages, PvP and the Discord link. Server-side, so there's nothing to install.

    CommandWhat it does
    /helpLists every command you're allowed to use
    /rulesShows the server rules
    /msg <player> <message>Private message. /tell and /w do the same
    /r <message>Reply to your last private message
    /ignore <player>Hides that player's chat, messages and item show-offs. Run again to undo
    /afkMarks you away. Run again, or move, to come back
    /sethome <name>Saves a home where you're standing
    /home <name>Teleports you to a home
    /homesLists your homes
    /delhome <name>Deletes a home
    /kit listShows the kits you can claim
    /kit <name>Claims a kit
    /pvp, /pvp on, /pvp offToggles your PvP. 30 seconds between switches, not within 15 seconds of a fight
    /phantoms, /phantoms on, /phantoms offTurns phantoms on or off for you
    /sidepanelHides or shows the side panel
    /voteClickable links to the five vote sites
    /linkGives you a code to link your Discord account. Send !link <code> to the bot within 10 minutes
    /link removeUnlinks your Discord account
    [item] in chatShows the item in your main hand. Others can hover over it. Shift + right-click an item in your inventory does the same

    Elements: Economy#

    Money, player shops and the auction house, all in Tenko coins. Server-side.

    CommandWhat it does
    /bal [player]Your balance, or someone else's
    /pay <player> <amount>Sends money
    /pay toggleStops or allows other players paying you
    /baltopThe richest players
    /paymentsYour recent payments
    /exchange deposit [amount]Turns coins from your inventory into balance
    /exchange withdraw <amount>Turns balance back into coins
    /shop create sellLook at a chest or barrel: makes it a shop that sells to players
    /shop create buyLook at a chest or barrel: makes it a shop that buys from players
    /shop rename <name>Renames the shop you're looking at
    /shop removeRemoves the shop you're looking at
    /ahOpens the auction house
    /ah sellLists the item in your hand
    /ah collectTakes everything out of your collection box
    /ah listingsYour active listings

    Elements: Claims#

    Land claiming with a Claim Deed, player ranks and sub-areas. Server-side.

    CommandWhat it does
    /claim deedGives you a Claim Deed (once every 30 minutes)
    /claimInside your claim: opens Land Config. Outside: your claim list
    /claim create <name>Starts a new property where you stand, if you have a free property slot
    /claim <property> manageMakes that property your active one
    /claim <property> transfer <player>Gives a property to another player
    /trust <player>Trusts a player in this claim, or in all of them if you're outside your land
    /trust <player> <subarea> <rank>Gives a player a rank inside one sub-area
    /untrust <player> [subarea]Removes a player from the claim, or from one sub-area
    /trustlistWho's trusted

    Codex: Title Scrolls#

    Scrolls found in loot unlock titles shown next to your name in chat. Titles you haven't found yet show as "?".

    CommandWhat it does
    /titleOpens your title collection. Click a title to wear it, click again to take it off

    Codex: Armor Stands#

    An armor stand editor that works through a tool and chest menus. Server-side.

    CommandWhat it does
    Hold Flint, right-clickOpens the editor menu: pose, rotate, move, scale, toggles, equipment, 40 preset poses, copy and paste, mirror
    Left / right-click a standOne step up or down in the current mode
    Sneak + scrollSwitches the axis (X, Y, Z)
    /armorstand infoOwner, lock and scale of the stand you're looking at. /as info works too

    Sit On Everything#

    Lets you sit on any block, mob or player. Server-side.

    CommandWhat it does
    /sitSit on the block, mob or player you're looking at. Press Shift to stand up

    Mods without commands#

    Some mods are used through clicks and keys instead. The ones people ask about most:

    ModHow it works
    PiggybackRight-click a crouching player with an empty hand to ride on their back.
    Carry OnSneak and right-click a chest, block or small mob with both hands empty to pick it up, contents and all. Right-click to put it down.
    WaystonesRight-click a Waystone to activate it, then right-click any Waystone to travel.
    Gravestone ModBreak your gravestone to get your items back. Only you can break it.
    ComfortsSleeping bags for the night and hammocks for the day. A sleeping bag doesn't change your spawn point.
    SupplementariesUse a Key on a safe or netherite door to lock it.
    Xaero's Minimap / World MapM opens the world map, B adds a waypoint. Cave mode is off on Tenko.
    JEIR over an item shows its recipe, U shows what it's used in.
    JadeShows what you're looking at at the top of the screen. No keys needed.
    Not here? /spawn, /tpa, /back, /warp and /nick are turned off for players on Tenko.
