    Coins & Shops

    Tenko's money is physical. Coins sit in your inventory like any other item, which means you can drop them, store them in a chest, or lose them if you aren't careful.

    The three coins#

    CoinWorthIn Bronze
    Bronze Coin11
    Silver Coin100 Bronze100
    Gold Coin100 Silver10,000

    Coins stack up to 50. A full stack of Gold is 500,000 Bronze, so carrying it all around isn't a great idea. Keep most of it in a chest at home.

    Converting coins#

    Clucky, Exchanger of Coins stands at spawn and converts in both directions:

    • 100 Bronze into 1 Silver, and 1 Silver back into 100 Bronze
    • 100 Silver into 1 Gold, and 1 Gold back into 100 Silver

    There's no fee. Right-click Clucky to open the trade screen.

    Your bank and payments#

    Besides the coins you carry, you have a bank. Coins in the bank stay on the server, so they can't be dropped or lost. The side panel on the right of your screen shows the coins you carry as Bronze, Silver and Gold. /sidepanel hides or shows it.

    • /exchange deposit [amount] [bronze|silver|gold] puts coins into your bank. Add the coin type after the amount to count in Bronze, Silver or Gold. Leave out the amount to deposit every coin you carry.
    • /exchange withdraw <amount> [bronze|silver|gold] takes coins back out of your bank. You get the fewest coins that add up to the amount.
    • /pay <player> <amount> sends money to another player. /pay toggle stops people sending you money.
    • /bal shows the coins you carry and your bank, /baltop the richest players, /payments your recent payments.

    Purchases are never announced in chat or on the Discord.

    Ways to earn#

    • Playtime rewards: every Rufus tier gives coins along with items. All 20 tiers add up to 1,155 Bronze, 231 Silver and 77 Gold. See Playtime & Voting.
    • Loot bags: mobs sometimes drop a loot bag with coins and items. The Wither, Ender Dragon and Warden always drop a Boss Loot Bag with 50 to 100 Gold Coins.
    • Selling to players: through your own chest shop, the auction house, or a straight trade.
    • Starter money: Natsubashi gives every new player 30 Bronze, 10 Silver and 2 Gold.

    The NPC shops only sell. They don't buy anything from players.

    NPC shops at spawn#

    The shopkeepers in the spawn houses sell decoration: furniture, lighting, storage blocks, plushies and so on. Prices run from 10 Bronze to 100 Gold, depending on how hard the item is to craft. Everyday items like ores, food and tools are never sold. Those have to be found, farmed or crafted.

    Player chest shops#

    Any chest or barrel on your own land can be a shop.

    1. Put the items you're selling in the chest (or leave it empty for a buy shop).
    2. Look at the chest and type /shop create sell to sell to players, or /shop create buy to buy from them.
    3. Set the item and price in the menu that opens. One chest can sell several items, each with its own price.

    A sign shows what the shop trades. Your sales go into the shop's till. /shop rename <name> names it and /shop remove takes it down. You get a message when something sells.

    Shops can't be emptied by hoppers, pipes or Create machines, and pistons and drills can't break them. A Create Item Vault can be linked to a shop as a stock vault so the shop restocks itself.

    Spud's Shops blocks also work, if you'd rather sell from a shop block.

    Auction house#

    /ah opens the auction house. Browse by category, search, buy outright, or bid.

    • Selling: hold the item and type /ah sell. Pick buy-now or auction, set the price and choose how long it runs: 1, 6, 12 or 24 hours.
    • Bidding: each bid has to be at least 5% above the last one.
    • Limits: up to 10 listings at a time.
    • Collecting: money from sales, items that didn't sell and items you won go to your collection box. /ah collect takes everything out, and /ah listings shows your own listings.
    • There's no sales tax.

    Typing prices#

    Prices in shops and the auction house are typed as one number in Bronze.

    PriceType
    10 Bronze10
    5 Silver500
    1 Gold10000 or 10k
    1 Gold 50 Silver15000
    3 Gold30k
    Don't add a coin letter. k means thousand and m means million. A b on the end means billion, not Bronze.
