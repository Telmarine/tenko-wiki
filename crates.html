    Crates & Keys

    Five crates sit at spawn. Each one opens with its own coin. Hold the coin and right-click the matching crate. Every crate has 40 possible prizes. The chances below are rounded.

    From common to rare#

    CrateOpened withWhere the coin comes from
    VoteVote CoinOne per vote, on each of the five vote sites
    Sapphire StarSapphire Star CoinLoot chests, boss loot bags, the Vote Crate
    Dark EmeraldDark Emerald CoinLoot chests, boss loot bags, the Vote and Sapphire Star Crates
    OnyxOnyx CoinLoot chests, boss loot bags, the Sapphire Star and Dark Emerald Crates
    TenkoTenko CoinLoot chests, boss loot bags, the Dark Emerald and Onyx Crates

    Crate coins aren't money. Clucky won't convert them and shops won't take them, but you can trade them with other players.

    Each boss kill rolls a crate coin from the Boss Loot Bag: Sapphire Star 40%, Dark Emerald 35%, Onyx 20%, Tenko 5%. See Bosses.

    Opens with a Vote Coin. The everyday crate. Mostly building and crafting materials, Create parts and iron gear, with a small shot at higher crate coins.
