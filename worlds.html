    Travel & Worlds

    Tenko has no teleport commands. Getting around is done with Waystones and homes, which keeps the world feeling big and makes builds along the way worth something.

    The worlds#

    WorldSizeWhat it's for
    SpawnPocket worldShops, NPCs, crates, WarpGates. Can't be claimed or built on.
    Overworld25,000 × 25,000The main world. Build, claim, settle.
    NetherBorder at 3,125The main Nether. Claims work here.
    The EndBorder at 10,000The main End and the Ender Dragon. Claims work here.
    Resource OverworldSeparate worldVanilla terrain for mining and gathering.
    Resource NetherSeparate worldVanilla Nether for gathering.
    Resource EndSeparate worldVanilla End for gathering.

    Leaving spawn#

    Jump into the water under the "Jump into action here!" sign. It drops you at a random spot in the main Overworld. Every jump lands somewhere new, so set a home once you find a place you like.

    Waystones#

    Waystones are found in villages and out in the wild, and you can craft your own. Right-click one to activate it. After that you can travel to it from any other Waystone, for free. You start with 2 Waystones in the starter kit, so you can put one at your base and one wherever you need it.

    Tenko Spawn is unlocked for everyone from the start. Place a Waystone, name it, and it becomes active. Right-click it and Tenko Spawn is on the list.

    Homes#

    • /sethome <name> saves where you're standing.
    • /home <name> takes you there.
    • /homes lists them, and /delhome <name> deletes one.

    You get 1 home as a Newcomer and one more per rank, up to 10. Homes can't be set in the resource worlds.

    Resource worlds#

    The WarpGates at spawn lead to three resource worlds: an Overworld, a Nether and an End. They're plain vanilla terrain, made for mining and gathering so the main world doesn't get stripped bare.

    • No claims, no homes and no Waystones.
    • To leave, walk back through a WarpGate or use /home.
    • Don't build anything you want to keep there.

    Bosses#

    The Wither, the Ender Dragon and the Warden each drop a Boss Loot Bag when killed:

    DropChance
    50 to 100 Gold CoinsAlways
    Diamond40%
    Netherite Scrap25%
    Enchanted Golden Apple15%
    Netherite Ingot10%
    Crate coin: Sapphire Star / Dark Emerald / Onyx / TenkoOne of these: 40 / 35 / 20 / 5%
    Title Scroll: Epic / Legendary / Unique35 / 10 / 1%

    The Ender Dragon in the main End comes back 30 minutes after it dies, and has a 35% chance to drop a Dragon Egg on every kill after the first.

    Dying#

    When you die, everything you carried goes into a gravestone at that spot. Go back and break it, and your items go straight back into your inventory. Only you and staff can break your gravestone.
