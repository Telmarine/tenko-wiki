    Claims

    Claimed land is protected. Nobody can break, place, open chests or use anything on it unless you let them. Claims are made one chunk (16 × 16 blocks, full height) at a time.

    The Claim Deed#

    Type /claim deed to get a Claim Deed. You can get a new one once every 30 minutes, so don't lose it.

    • Right-click with the Deed to claim the chunk you're standing in.
    • Sneak and right-click to unclaim it.
    • Right-click inside your own claim to open Land Config.

    Claims work in the main Overworld, Nether and End. The resource worlds and spawn can't be claimed.

    Claim outlines#

    While you hold the Deed, nearby claims get a thin outline at your feet:

    ColorMeaning
    GreenYour claims
    Light blueClaims you're trusted in
    RedOther players' claims
    OrangeAdmin claims
    YellowSub-areas
    WhiteThe unclaimed chunk you're standing in

    How much you can claim#

    Newcomers get 100 chunks. Each rank adds 100, up to 1,000 at Tenko. See Ranks & Limits.

    There has to be a gap of at least 2 chunks between your claim and another player's. Claims don't expire.

    Properties#

    All the land you claim belongs to your property. It has its own name, ranks and flags. Every rank can own 1 property, and your chunk allowance covers everything in it, across the Overworld, Nether and End.

    • /claim <property> transfer <player> gives a property to another player. They have to be online, have enough free chunks, and accept. Claims doesn't move money, so agree on the price first.

    Trusting players#

    • /trust <player> lets someone use your land. Run it inside a claim to trust them in that claim only, or outside your claims to trust them everywhere.
    • /untrust <player> removes them.
    • /trustlist shows who's trusted.

    Land Config: ranks, flags and bans#

    Type /claim inside your claim (or right-click with the Deed) to open Land Config. Outside your claims, /claim shows your claim list.

    • Ranks: make up to 10 ranks, like Friend, Builder or Shop Helper, and choose exactly what each one can do: build, open chests, use doors, use machines, hit animals and more.
    • Admin Mode: give one rank Admin Mode and its members can trust, untrust, promote, demote and ban anyone ranked below them. Only you see this toggle.
    • Flags: rules for the land itself, such as explosions, fire spread and what visitors can do.
    • Bans: a banned player is removed from every list in the claim and can't enter it. If they're inside, they get moved just outside the border.

    Sub-areas#

    A sub-area is a smaller zone inside your claim with its own player list, like a shop stall or a friend's house in your town. Up to 20 per claim.

    1. Open Land Config, go to Sub-areas and take the Sub-area Stick.
    2. Right-click two corners to mark the area.
    3. Give players a rank just inside it: /trust <player> <subarea> <rank>. Names with spaces go in quotes: /trust Steve "Shop 1" "Shop Owner".
    4. /untrust <player> <subarea> takes them out of the sub-area. Their rank in the rest of the claim stays.

    You can pick one player as a sub-area's manager. They can add, remove and rank players there while you're away, but only with ranks below their own.

    If you remove someone who still has blocks in a sub-area, they get 7 days to collect their things. After that, or once they've taken everything, whatever's left is yours.

    Locks and keys#

    For extra safety inside your own base, use a Key on a safe or a netherite door to lock it. After that it only opens for a player holding a matching key. Rename the key in an anvil to tell them apart.
