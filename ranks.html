    Ranks & Limits

    Ranks are earned by voting, and only by voting. They can't be bought. The names count the tails of the fox, from one tail up to the nine-tailed Tenko.

    The rank ladder#

    Votes are your all-time total. You move up the moment your total reaches the next rank, even if you're offline, and the promotion is announced in game and on the Discord.

    What a rank gives you#

    • One more home per rank, from 1 as a Newcomer to 10 at Tenko.
    • 100 more claim chunks per rank, from 100 to 1,000.
    • A name color in chat, the tab list and the side panel.
    • A Discord role to match, once your account is linked with /link.

    Every rank can own 1 property (one connected area of claimed land). See Properties.

    If you're wearing a title from a Title Scroll, the side panel shows your name in the title's color instead.

    Staff ranks#

    Staff are called the Chosen Ones. From the top: Owner, Sr Admin, Admin, Sr Mod, Mod and Helper. Ask any of them for help. Staff ranks aren't vote ranks and can't be earned by voting.
