How Tenko works

The server's own systems. Everything here is found or earned in the world. Nothing is pay-to-win.

Coins

Money is physical: Bronze, Silver and Gold Coins you carry and trade. Spend them at the NPC shops in the spawn houses or in other players' shops.

Clucky at spawn swaps 100 Bronze for 1 Silver and 100 Silver for 1 Gold, and back.

Crates & keys

Five crates sit at spawn, each opened with its own coin. Keys come from voting, Lootr chests, rare mob drops and boss kills.

Vote CoinSapphire Star CoinDark Emerald CoinOnyx CoinTenko Coin

Voting & ranks

Type /vote for the five vote sites. Each vote gives a Vote Coin, and votes add up to new ranks, from Ichibi up to Tenko. Every rank adds one more home.

Title Scrolls

Scrolls found in loot unlock chat titles, from common to legendary. Pick the one you show with /title.

Travel

Waystones carry you between places you've found. Set homes with /sethome and return with /home.

WarpGates at spawn lead to the three resource worlds.

Resource worlds

Separate Overworld, Nether and End worlds with plain vanilla generation, made for mining and gathering. They can't be claimed, and homes and Waystones don't work there. Leave through the WarpGates or /home.

Boss loot

The Wither, the Ender Dragon and the Warden each drop a Boss Loot Bag, a chance at crate keys and a chance at an Epic, Legendary or Unique Title Scroll.

The Ender Dragon can also drop its egg on repeat kills.

Playtime rewards

Rufus at spawn hands out rewards for time played. Time spent AFK doesn't count.

Claims

Hold a Claim Deed and right-click to claim the chunk you're standing in. Sneak + right-click unclaims it. Lost your Deed? /claim deed gives you a new one.

Share your land with /trust <player>. Type /claim inside your claim to set ranks, flags and sub-areas.

PvP

PvP is on. Turn it off for yourself with /pvp (30-second cooldown). You can't toggle it in the 15 seconds after a fight.

Sitting & riding

Sit on almost any block with /sit. Piggyback lets you carry another player.

Help in game

Natsubashi at spawn gives every new player the Book of Knowledge and a starter kit. /rules and /help cover the basics.

Tenko's Own Mods

6 mods

Built for Tenko by TAC5 Studios. Mods marked server run on the server only, so there's nothing extra to install.

Title Scrolls

Scrolls found in loot unlock chat titles you can show next to your name. Choose yours with /title.

Elements: Nexus server

Runs ranks, chat, the tab list and the side panel, plus homes, kits, /pvp, /phantoms, /vote and the Discord chat link. Show off an item in chat with [item] or by shift + right-clicking it in your inventory.

Elements: Economy server

Adds the auction house (/ah) and chest shops (look at a chest and type /shop create buy or /shop create sell), all paid in Tenko coins. See your trade history with /payments.

Elements: Claims server

Claim land with a Claim Deed and trust friends into ranks you set up yourself. /claim opens the Land Config menu for ranks, flags and sub-areas. Hold the Deed to see claim outlines.

Elements: Vault server

Lets shop and coin mods share one economy. Comes bundled with Elements: Economy.

Elements: Armor Stands server

Hold flint and right-click to open the editor: pose, move and rotate stands, show arms, change size, swap their gear, or pick one of 40 ready-made poses. Works on stands you placed.
