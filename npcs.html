    NPCs

    Everyone you'll meet at spawn, and what they're there for. Right-click any of them to talk or trade.

    Guides#

    NPCWhat they do
    Natsubashi, The Tenko GuardianA divine fox and the keeper of spawn. Gives every new player the Book of Knowledge, the starter kit and their starting coins.
    BethWelcomes players at spawn.
    Rufus, The TimekeepaHands out playtime rewards. See Playtime rewards.
    Clucky, Exchanger of CoinsConverts Bronze, Silver and Gold up and down. See Converting coins.
    Tyler, Day 1 LegendHas the Day 1 kit for early players. It won't be around forever, so grab it while you still can.
